at. Replace it. Citations work through natbib in every venue mode, both
parenthetical \citep{vaswani2017attention} and textual
\citet{kingma2015adam}.

Cross-references, math, and the usual environments behave as they always
do:
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:softmax}
  \mathrm{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V .
\end{equation}

\begin{definition}[Shared preamble]
  \label{def:preamble}
  A preamble is \emph{shared} when changing it once changes every build of the
  paper.
\end{definition}
