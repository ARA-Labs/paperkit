Every ML paper starts by copying a preamble from the last one, and every
collaborator ends up with a slightly different copy. \method{} collapses that
preamble into a single style package: one option switches the venue, one macro
renders the first page, and the rest of the file is the paper. This document is
the template and the demo at once, and it compiles unchanged as an arXiv
preprint, a NeurIPS submission, or an ICML submission.
